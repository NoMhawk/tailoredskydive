Best Skydive in Devon: How to Choose the Right Tandem

Everyone wants the best skydive, but “best” depends on what matters to you. This page sets out what to check before you book anywhere, and how a one-to-one tandem with Rich Taylor at Dunkeswell Airfield in Devon measures up. 10,000ft from £275, 15,000ft from £300.

What does “best” really mean?

Be wary of any centre that simply calls itself the best. It is a claim nobody can prove, because a great skydive is partly about the day, the weather and how you feel. A better question is which centre is best for you, and that comes down to a few things you can check.

Seven things to check before you book

1. Is it properly regulated?

Ask whether the operator and its instructors are part of British Skydiving, the sport's national body. Tailored Skydive uses equipment maintained to British Skydiving standards.

2. Who will actually jump with you?

Ask who your instructor is and how much you will see of them before the jump. With us it is Rich Taylor, from your first message to your debrief.

3. How is the equipment backed up?

Every tandem system should have a main parachute, an independent reserve and an automatic activation device. Ours do.

4. Will you be rushed?

Some centres run a high-volume production line. Ours is one-to-one, with an unhurried briefing at your pace. See our guide for nervous first-time skydivers.

5. What is the full price?

Check what is included, and what costs extra, before you compare. Ours is 10,000ft for £275 or 15,000ft for £300, with photo and video optional from £125.

6. What height and view do you get?

More height means more freefall. See the 15,000ft tandem skydive for how heights and times compare. On a clear day the view stretches from Weymouth to Wales.

7. What do independent reviews say?

Read recent reviews on sites the centre does not control, such as Google, and look for detail rather than only star ratings.

Ask us any of these before you book. Rich replies to booking requests within 24 hours, and you can reach him on WhatsApp or by email.

Best skydive FAQs

What is the best skydive in Devon?

The best skydive is the one that suits you, and nobody can honestly name one winner for everybody. Use the checklist on this page to compare centres on safety, who you jump with, what is included and the total price.

Is Tailored Skydive the best skydive in Devon?

We would rather you judge for yourself than take our word for it. What we offer is a one-to-one tandem with Rich Taylor, a calm unhurried briefing, and clear pricing. Ask us the questions on this page before you book.

Is a more expensive skydive better?

Not necessarily. Compare what the price includes: the height, photo and video, and any extra fees. Our 10,000ft tandem is £275 and our 15,000ft tandem is £300, with photo and video optional from £125.

Which height is best for a first skydive?

A 10,000ft jump gives about 30 seconds of freefall and a long canopy ride, and is a great first skydive. A 15,000ft jump gives up to about 60 seconds of freefall for £25 more.

How do I check a skydive centre is reputable?

Ask whether the operator and instructors are part of British Skydiving, ask how the equipment is maintained, and read recent independent reviews on sites you do not control.

Who can jump?

You must be at least 16 (under 18s need written consent), the weight limit is approximately 90kg (around 14 stone) fully clothed, and you complete the British Skydiving medical declaration.
