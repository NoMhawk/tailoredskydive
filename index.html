Meet Your Instructor

Hi, I'm Rich Taylor.

My skydiving journey started right here at Dunkeswell with a tandem jump given to me as a birthday present. Just a few months later, I decided this wasn't going to be a one-off—I wanted to become an instructor myself. Since 2012, that decision has turned into over 3,500 jumps and more than 56 hours of pure freefall time.

When I’m not in the air paragliding, you’ll usually find me out on the water either surfing, kitesurfing, wing foiling, or paddleboarding, or keeping active with golf and pickleball.

Why Jump With Me? A Personal, Unhurried Experience

For me, skydiving isn't a conveyor-belt job. I want to share your once-in-a-lifetime experience and make it as memorable as possible.

Whether you're here purely for the adrenaline and fun, or facing down a major fear, my goal is to keep you safe, relaxed, and laughing throughout the whole experience. Through Tailored Skydive, you get a true 1-to-1, unhurried journey where I am personally by your side every step of the way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your luxury tandem skydive experience with Rich Taylor at Dunkeswell.

A tandem skydive is the ultimate way to experience freefall with minimal training. You are securely attached via a professional dual-harness system to an experienced instructor who manages all the equipment, deployment, and landing, allowing you to simply absorb the incredible experience.
None at all. Before your jump, I will walk you through a thorough, one-to-one briefing that covers everything you need to know about body position, aircraft exit, and what to expect during the flight.
You must be at least 16 years old to skydive. Anyone under 18 requires written parental or guardian consent. There is no upper age limit, provided you meet the medical fitness requirements.
Yes, for safety and equipment operational limits, the maximum weight limit is approximately 90 kg (around 14 stone), fully clothed including shoes.
You should wear comfortable, weather-appropriate clothing that allows for free movement—such as jeans or joggers and a t-shirt or fleece depending on the season. Because it is significantly cooler at altitude, dressing in comfortable layers works best. We will provide you with an outer tandem jumpsuit, harness, and goggles. For footwear, please wear supportive, flat trainers or lace-up sports shoes (strictly no boots with hooks, sandals, or high heels). If you wear glasses or contact lenses, that's no problem at all—just let me know beforehand so we can ensure they are securely fastened or fit comfortably beneath your goggles.
All tandem students must complete the British Skydiving medical documentation. If you meet all the self-declaration criteria on Form 115a, you can sign it yourself. If you have any listed medical conditions or previous injuries, you will need Form 115b signed and stamped by your doctor prior to jumping.
Your day is structured, thorough, and personal. The process runs from arrival and sign-in, through your paperwork and one-to-one safety briefing, to a short wait for your flight slot. From there, you will get kitted up in your jumpsuit and harness, do a quick pre-jump video interview, run through final flight line checks, and board the plane. After the jump, freefall, and canopy ride, we touch down for a post-jump debrief, de-kit, and final wrap-up.

A lot of people expect that dropping stomach-drop sensation you get on a theme park ride, but you actually won't feel that at all. Because you're exiting an aircraft moving at high forward speed, freefall feels more like floating on a massive, powerful cushion of air rather than dropping downward. It's exhilarating, incredibly freeing, and completely surreal.
Being nervous is completely normal—it’s a natural human reaction to doing something extraordinary! Because you booked a bespoke, personal experience rather than a high-volume assembly line, we will move at your pace, talk through every step, and make sure you feel entirely comfortable and confident before getting on the plane.
We use state-of-the-art dual-student tandem equipment meticulously maintained to strict British Skydiving standards. Every system includes a main parachute, an independent reserve parachute, and an Automatic Activation Device (AAD)—a computerized backup unit that will automatically deploy the reserve parachute if parameters are met.
Yes, you can breathe normally through your nose or mouth—the rush of air is powerful, but it doesn't stop you from breathing. Freefall is loud due to the wind rushing past at around 120 mph, so verbal conversation happens once the parachute opens and we're flying under canopy. Once the parachute opens by 5,000 feet, the roaring rush of freefall instantly transitions into a peaceful, quiet glide. If you're feeling adventurous, we can ramp up the excitement with some turns, or we can just cruise and enjoy the scenery.

If you want personal medical, life, or accident coverage specifically for skydiving, you may want to check your existing policies or look into extreme sports insurance providers beforehand.
