Meet Your Instructor

Hi, I's Rich Taylor.

My own skydiving journey started right here at Dunkeswell with my very first tandem jump! Since then, I’ve accumulated over 3,500 skydives jumping worldwide, alongside a lifelong passion for paragliding, surfing, golf, and pickleball. As a multi-medallist across multiple national sports including skydiving, elite performance and precision are in everything I do.

Dunkeswell features views over Devon that are truly some of the best in the country, and the relaxed Devon vibe here is second to none. Through Tailored Skydive, I offer a bespoke, personal 1-to-1 experience for jumpers who want an unhurried, deeply custom journey where I am personally with you every single step of the way.

A note on value: Because this is a luxury service, if cost is your primary factor, it is much cheaper to book directly with skydivesouthwest.com! But if you are looking for a hand-crafted, exclusive 1-to-1 experience where I personally guide you from briefing to landing, let's make it happen.
