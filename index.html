Meet Your Instructor

"Skydiving isn't just an adrenaline rush—it's a life-changing experience."

Hi, I’m Rich Taylor. Over my years instructing across the UK, I noticed something missing in commercial skydiving: personal connection. First-time jumpers were often treated like a ticket number, rushed through gear checks with little time to settle pre-jump nerves.

That’s why I founded TailoredSkydive.com. When you book with me, you aren't handed off to strangers. From our first chat, through your tailored ground brief, to stepping out the door at 15,000 feet—I am your dedicated instructor, guide, and pilot in command of your safety.
