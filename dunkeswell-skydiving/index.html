About Dunkeswell Airfield

Dunkeswell Airfield sits in East Devon near the eastern edge of the Blackdown Hills, about 5 miles north of Honiton and 16 miles north-east of Exeter. Today it is a busy civilian airfield used for general aviation, flight training, and skydiving.

It also has a serious history. Built in 1942 and 1943 as RAF Dunkeswell, it was handed to American forces, and from late 1943 the US Navy flew antisubmarine patrols from here during the Second World War. After the war it returned to civilian hands. The Dunkeswell Heritage Centre at the airfield tells that story; check its opening times before you plan a visit.

The view from the air

The climb to altitude takes roughly 15 to 20 minutes over the Devon and Somerset countryside. On a clear day the view stretches from Weymouth to Wales, taking in both the north and south coastlines. Then the door opens, freefall begins, and your parachute opens so you can enjoy a quiet glide back to the airfield.

Dunkeswell skydiving FAQs

Where is Dunkeswell Airfield?

Dunkeswell Airfield is in East Devon, near the village of Dunkeswell and the eastern edge of the Blackdown Hills. It is about 5 miles north of Honiton and 16 miles north-east of Exeter.

Who do I jump with at Dunkeswell?

Tailored Skydive is Rich Taylor's own one-to-one tandem service. Rich jumps with Skydive South West, the separate centre based at the airfield, and you deal directly with Rich from your first message to your debrief.

How much is a tandem skydive at Dunkeswell?

A 10,000ft tandem skydive is £275 and a 15,000ft jump is £300. Photo and video are optional extras: £125 for the handycam selfie package or £200 for the ultimate package.

Where do I meet you?

Every jump is at Dunkeswell Airfield. Rich confirms the meeting point and arrival time in writing when you book, so you will have it before the day.

What should I wear?

Comfortable layers and flat, lace-up shoes. It is cooler at altitude. A jumpsuit, harness and goggles are provided.

Who can jump?

You must be at least 16 (under 18s need written consent), the weight limit is approximately 90kg (around 14 stone) fully clothed, and you complete the British Skydiving medical declaration.
