Is 15,000ft really the highest skydive in the UK?

For tandem skydiving, yes: 15,000ft is the highest altitude generally offered in the UK. It is also offered by a small number of other centres, so if you compare operators you will see the same headline height. We would rather tell you that plainly than pretend it is unique.

So the useful question is what else you get at 15,000ft. With Tailored Skydive that is a personal jump: Rich Taylor guides you from your first message to your debrief, the briefing is one-to-one and unhurried, and the pace is yours. Read more about Rich and how the experience works.

How far is the fall? A side-on view

Whatever height you jump from, your parachute is open by 5,000ft. That means the extra height you climb is all freefall. The diagram below shows each exit height drawn to scale, with the blue bar showing the freefall before the parachute opens.

Climbing from 10,000ft to 15,000ft doubles the distance you fall before the parachute opens, and roughly doubles the time. The climb to 10,000ft or 15,000ft takes roughly 15 to 20 minutes.

What a 15,000ft skydive is like

The jump aircraft is a turbine-powered Cessna 208B Grand Caravan, and the scenic climb to 15,000ft takes roughly 15 to 20 minutes over the Devon and Somerset countryside. On a clear day the view stretches from Weymouth to Wales, taking in both the north and south coastlines.

Then the door opens. Freefall is loud, with the wind rushing past at around 120mph, so Rich uses hand signals. It does not feel like a rollercoaster drop. People describe it as floating on a powerful cushion of air. Up to about a minute later your parachute opens by 5,000ft, the noise stops, and you glide down, chatting, enjoying the view and, if there is time and it is safe, steering the canopy yourself.

It is cooler at altitude, so wear comfortable layers and flat, lace-up shoes. A jumpsuit, harness and goggles are provided.

Is it worth going higher?

A 15,000ft jump is £300 against £275 at 10,000ft, so you pay £25 more for roughly double the freefall. If you are torn between going higher and paying for video, Rich's advice is to go higher. If your budget stretches, get both: a 15,000ft jump with the ultimate package (handycam selfie plus an outside camera, £200) comes to £500. Build your jump and see the total.

You can also choose your style: a calm, reassuring pace, a balanced jump, or a high-energy one with faster canopy turns for thrill-seekers. Nervous about the height? That is completely normal, and the one-to-one pace means nothing is rushed.

Who can do a 15,000ft skydive?

The requirements are the same as for any tandem skydive. You must be at least 16, with written consent if you are under 18. There is no upper age limit as long as you meet the medical fitness requirements, and the weight limit is approximately 90kg (around 14 stone) fully clothed. Everyone completes the British Skydiving medical declaration: Form 115a if you can self-declare, or Form 115b signed by your doctor if you have a listed condition or past injury.

15,000ft skydive FAQs

Is 15,000ft the highest skydive in the UK?

15,000ft is the highest altitude generally offered for tandem skydives in the UK. A small number of centres offer it, including Tailored Skydive at Dunkeswell Airfield, so the height itself is the same. How long is the freefall from 15,000ft?

Up to about 60 seconds, roughly double the 30 seconds you get from 10,000ft. You fall at around 120mph, then your parachute opens by 5,000ft and you glide down quietly.

How much more does a 15,000ft skydive cost than 10,000ft?

A 15,000ft tandem skydive is £300 and a 10,000ft jump is £275, so going higher costs £25 more. Photo and video are optional extras: £125 for the handycam selfie package or £200 for the ultimate package.

Is a 15,000ft skydive scarier than a lower one?

Not in the way most people expect. Because you leave an aircraft that is already moving fast, freefall feels more like floating on a cushion of air than the stomach-drop of a theme park ride. Rich talks you through every sensation before it happens, so nerves are normal and you move at your own pace.

Who can do a 15,000ft tandem skydive?

The same requirements apply as for any tandem skydive: you must be at least 16 (under 18s need written consent), the weight limit is approximately 90kg (around 14 stone) fully clothed, and you complete the British Skydiving medical declaration.

Is it worth going higher, or should I pay for video instead?

Rich's advice is to go higher. The freefall from altitude is the part you will remember, and it is only £25 more. If your budget allows, get both: a 15,000ft jump with the ultimate media package is £500 in total.
