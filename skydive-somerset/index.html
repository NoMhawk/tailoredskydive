Skydive Somerset: Tandem Skydiving Just Over the Border

If you are looking for a skydive in Somerset, here is the straight answer: our jumps launch from Dunkeswell Airfield in East Devon, close to the Blackdown Hills, which run across the Devon and Somerset border. Tailored Skydive offers one-to-one tandem skydives with instructor Rich Taylor, at a pace that suits you.

Is the skydive in Somerset?

Not quite, and we would rather say so than leave you to find out on the way. Dunkeswell Airfield is in East Devon, near the eastern edge of the Blackdown Hills and a few miles north of Honiton. That landscape runs across the Devon and Somerset border, which is why people on the Somerset side can reach us for a day out.

We have not quoted drive times, because they depend on where in Somerset you are starting. Put “Dunkeswell Airfield” into your satnav to see your own route, and Rich will confirm the meeting point and arrival time in writing when you book.

Why travel for a one-to-one skydive?

A tandem skydive is a big moment, and many people want it to feel personal rather than processed. With Tailored Skydive you deal with one person from your first message to your debrief. Rich Taylor answers your questions, talks you through the briefing without rushing, and jumps with you. You can choose a calm, reassuring pace, a balanced jump, or a high-energy one with faster canopy turns.

The view over Devon and Somerset

The climb to altitude takes roughly 15 to 20 minutes over the Devon and Somerset countryside. On a clear day the view stretches from Weymouth to Wales, taking in both the north and south coastlines. Then the door opens, freefall begins, and a few moments later your parachute opens and the noise drops away so you can enjoy a quiet glide down.

It is cooler at altitude, so wear comfortable layers and flat, lace-up shoes. A jumpsuit, harness and goggles are provided.

Skydive Somerset FAQs

Is there a tandem skydive in Somerset?

Our tandem skydives take place at Dunkeswell Airfield in East Devon, not in Somerset itself. The airfield sits near the eastern edge of the Blackdown Hills, an area that stretches across the Devon and Somerset border, so it is a realistic day out if you live in Somerset.

Where exactly do I meet you?

Every jump is at Dunkeswell Airfield in Devon. Rich confirms the meeting point and arrival time when you book, so you will have it in writing before the day.

Will I fly over Somerset?

Quite possibly. The climb to altitude takes roughly 15 to 20 minutes over the Devon and Somerset countryside, and on a clear day the view stretches from Weymouth to Wales. What you see depends on the weather and the direction of the climb on the day.

How much is a tandem skydive?

A 10,000ft tandem skydive is £275 and a 15,000ft jump is £300. Photo and video are optional extras: £125 for the handycam selfie package or £200 for the ultimate package.

Who can jump?

You must be at least 16 (under 18s need written consent), the weight limit is approximately 90kg (around 14 stone) fully clothed, and you complete the British Skydiving medical declaration.
