Who we are and where we jump

Tailored Skydive is Rich Taylor's own one-to-one tandem service. Our jumps take place at Dunkeswell Airfield in East Devon, where Rich jumps with Skydive South West, the separate centre based at the airfield. We are a different business, and we would rather you know exactly who you are booking with.

What we add is the personal side. You deal with one person from your first message to your debrief, the briefing is unhurried, and you choose a calm, balanced or high-energy jump. Read more about Rich or how the experience works.

A skydive for the whole South West

The South West is a big region, and a skydive is a day trip for many people in it, whether you live here or are visiting on holiday. We have not quoted drive times, because they depend on where you are starting. Put “Dunkeswell Airfield” into your satnav to see your own route.

Starting from Somerset? See our page on skydiving near Somerset. For the full day, from arrival to landing, read our guide to skydiving in Devon, and if you want the longest freefall see the 15,000ft tandem skydive.

The view from the air

The climb to altitude takes roughly 15 to 20 minutes over the Devon and Somerset countryside. On a clear day the view stretches from Weymouth to Wales, taking in both the north and south coastlines. Then the door opens, freefall begins, and your parachute opens so you can enjoy a quiet glide down.

It is cooler at altitude, so wear comfortable layers and flat, lace-up shoes. A jumpsuit, harness and goggles are provided.

Skydive South West FAQs

Where can I skydive in the South West of England?

Tailored Skydive runs one-to-one tandem skydives from Dunkeswell Airfield in East Devon. Rich Taylor jumps with Skydive South West, the centre based at the airfield.

Is Tailored Skydive the same as Skydive South West?

No. Tailored Skydive is Rich Taylor's own one-to-one tandem service. Skydive South West is the separate centre based at Dunkeswell Airfield, and it is who Rich jumps with. Booking with us means you deal directly with Rich from your first message to your debrief.

Which part of the South West do you cover?

Every jump takes place at Dunkeswell Airfield in Devon. People travel to it from across the region, and Rich confirms the meeting point and arrival time in writing when you book.

How much is a tandem skydive?

A 10,000ft tandem skydive is £275 and a 15,000ft jump is £300. Photo and video are optional extras: £125 for the handycam selfie package or £200 for the ultimate package.

What if the weather is bad on the day?

Skydiving depends on the weather, so a jump can be postponed. Rich will talk you through your options if that happens.

Who can jump?

You must be at least 16 (under 18s need written consent), the weight limit is approximately 90kg (around 14 stone) fully clothed, and you complete the British Skydiving medical declaration.

Ready to book your skydive?

Pick your height, style and photo package, and Rich will get back to you within 24 hours.
